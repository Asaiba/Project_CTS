End User Licence Agreement (EULA)

This End User Licence Agreement is a legal agreement between you and SAIBA Aubin, developer of the College Tokenized System ("CTS" or the "Platform"). It governs access to the CTS web application, its APIs, and the associated smart contracts deployed on the Ethereum Sepolia test network.

Important notice

CTS uses blockchain transactions for proposal creation, DAO offers, offer selection, funding, and withdrawals. Once a blockchain transaction is confirmed, CTS cannot reverse it.

Clause 1: Acceptance

By registering an account, connecting a wallet, submitting a scholarship essay or application, creating a proposal, sending a DAO offer or pass response, selecting a DAO offer, funding a proposal, withdrawing funds, or otherwise using CTS, you confirm that you have read, understood, and agree to be bound by this Agreement. If you do not agree, you must not use the Platform.

Why this matters: informed consent is required before CTS processes personal data or initiates irreversible blockchain interactions on your behalf.

Clause 2: Licence Grant

Subject to this Agreement, CTS grants you a limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable, revocable licence to access and use the Platform only for the following purposes:

  • submitting student scholarship essays and applications;
  • reviewing student applications and creating school-fee proposals as a registered college user;
  • participating as a DAO member by submitting an offer or recording a pass response;
  • selecting offers, funding proposals, and withdrawing claimable funds as permitted by the Platform; and
  • managing the system as an authorised administrator.

No other use is permitted, including scraping platform data, automating wallet actions without authorisation, or using CTS APIs outside the scholarship lifecycle.

Clause 3: Blockchain Interactions and Irreversibility

You acknowledge that certain actions on CTS trigger blockchain transactions on the Ethereum Sepolia test network and may, in future production versions, trigger transactions on Ethereum mainnet or another Ethereum-compatible network. Once confirmed on-chain, these transactions are irreversible. CTS cannot reverse, modify, or cancel a confirmed blockchain transaction.

This includes, without limitation, wallet registration, proposal creation, DAO offer responses, student offer selection, funding disbursements, and fund withdrawals.

Why this matters: blockchain immutability is one of the core ethical and operational risks of the Platform and must be understood before any on-chain action is taken.

Clause 4: Wallet Address and Blockchain Identity

By registering on CTS, you acknowledge that your Ethereum wallet address may be stored in the CTS off-chain PostgreSQL database and may also be registered as an on-chain identifier in the CTS smart contract. CTS does not publish your name, email address, or institutional affiliation on-chain, but your wallet address and transaction history may still be publicly visible through blockchain explorers.

While a wallet's current registration status in the contract may later be updated by authorised owner actions, the historical on-chain record of your blockchain activity remains permanent and immutable.

Why this matters: a wallet address is a pseudonymous public identifier, and linking it to your real-world identity inside CTS creates a persistent audit trail.

Clause 5: Prohibited Uses

You agree not to:

  • create multiple accounts or wallets to obtain unfair influence or platform access;
  • submit fraudulent, misleading, or misrepresentative scholarship essays, applications, or institutional records;
  • use bots, scripts, or automated tooling to interact with the Platform or smart contract without express permission;
  • attempt to exploit, manipulate, or circumvent the contract logic or the off-chain application controls; or
  • use CTS for any purpose that violates applicable law, including Rwanda's Law No. 058/2021 on data protection.

Clause 6: Limitation of Liability

To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, CTS and its developer shall not be liable for loss of funds resulting from smart contract vulnerabilities, blockchain network failures, user wallet compromise, private key mishandling, service interruptions, or third-party infrastructure failure. The Platform is provided on an "as is" basis for educational and research purposes.

Why this matters: CTS is currently a research and demonstration platform operating on test infrastructure and should not be presented as a formally audited production financial system.

Clause 7: Termination

CTS may suspend or terminate your account if you violate this Agreement, including by creating multiple accounts, submitting fraudulent information, or attempting to manipulate the Platform or smart contract. Termination affects your off-chain access to CTS but does not erase the immutable on-chain record of earlier transactions.

Clause 8: Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Republic of Rwanda. Any disputes arising from this Agreement shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the courts of Rwanda.
